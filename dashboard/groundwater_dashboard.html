    Source: Atal Bhujal Yojana disclosed groundwater level data, 2015–2022, data.gov.in (Ministry of Jal Shakti). Depth is metres below ground, so a bigger number means deeper water. Risk classes are depth bands set by the project author, not official CGWB categories: Safe up to 10 m, Watch 10–20 m, Critical 20–40 m, Severe over 40 m or dry. Change is the 2021 minus 2015 pre-monsoon depth, and recovery is the average fall in depth after the monsoon.
